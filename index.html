Hi, I am
Sushant Bhattarai A PhD Student A Research Assistant

I’m a PhD student in Computer Science at Kent State University and a Graduate Research Assistant in the SCALE Lab, where I work with Dr. Gokarna Sharma. I study how distributed systems can use imperfect predictions about which nodes will request a shared object. My MS thesis, Online Learning-Augmented Distributed Directories, examined how those predictions can guide communication when they become available over time.

I’m also studying online routing with multiple repairpersons on a line. In this problem, each new request raises two questions: which repairperson should serve it, and where should that repairperson go next? Before joining Kent State, I taught computer science at Himalaya College of Engineering and Sagarmatha Engineering College in Nepal.
